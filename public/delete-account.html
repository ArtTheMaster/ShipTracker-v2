Delete Your Account

Effective date: October 30, 2026

You may request deletion of your GJandAsher ShipTracker account and the personal information associated with it. This page explains how to make a request and what happens next.

To request deletion, email artlorence.hveridiano@gmail.com with the subject line “Account Deletion Request.”

What to include in your request

  • Your full name.
  • The email address registered to your ShipTracker account.
  • Your account role (customer, staff, administrator, or owner).
  • A clear statement that you want your account and personal data deleted. You may also tell us the reason, but it is optional.

How we verify and process requests

We may ask for additional information to verify that you own the account before deleting it. This protects your account from unauthorized deletion requests. Once verified, we will process the request within 30 days.

What we delete

After verification, we will delete or anonymize:

  • Your account profile and login record.
  • Your name, email address, contact number, and role.
  • Your staff approval or consent records, where no longer required.
  • Other personal information that is not required for a legal or operational purpose.

What we may retain

We may retain certain records when required by law or necessary for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Tax, accounting, and commercial bookkeeping records.
  • Order, delivery, return, and warranty records needed to resolve disputes.
  • Records needed to prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Records required by a court, regulator, or applicable legal process.

Retained records are limited to what is necessary and are protected under our privacy and security practices. Backup copies may take additional time to expire.

Before you delete your account

  • Deleting your account does not automatically cancel pending orders. Contact us separately if you need to cancel or discuss an active order.
  • If you are a staff member, your dispatch and return records may remain attributed to your former account for audit and operational continuity.
  • If you are the only owner or administrator, contact us so we can help arrange continued access for the business before deletion.

Questions

For questions about account deletion or your personal information, contact artlorence.hveridiano@gmail.com. You can also review our Privacy Policy.
