Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GJandAsher Digital Printing (“GJandAsher,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information through the GJandAsher ShipTracker mobile application and related services (the “Service”).

ShipTracker is an internal operations and customer-order management tool for customized motorcycle plate orders. It is used by authorized staff, business owners, administrators, and customers of GJandAsher Digital Printing.

By creating an account or using the Service, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information we collect

Account and identity information

  • Full name, email address, and contact number.
  • Account role (customer, staff, administrator, or owner).
  • For Google Sign-In: your Google display name, email address, and profile information made available by Google. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Staff account details, including approval status, reviewer, review timestamp, and the timestamp of your consent to this policy.

Order, delivery, and logistics information

  • Customer name, contact number, delivery address, and order or plate customization details.
  • Parcel and courier information such as tracking numbers, marketplace order references, courier status, dispatch records, delivery outcomes, and returns.
  • Customer QR codes and related identifiers used to retrieve customer records for walk-in or repeat orders.

Technical and usage information

  • Limited device and app information, such as device type, operating system, app version, and diagnostic or crash information.
  • Authentication and security logs, such as sign-in events and access attempts, used to protect accounts and the Service.

2. How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage customer and staff accounts.
  • Review and approve staff registration requests.
  • Process, fulfill, dispatch, track, and resolve customized motorcycle plate orders.
  • Coordinate with couriers and handle delivery, return, and replacement cases.
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • Generate customer QR codes and maintain accurate customer records.
  • Produce operational analytics, delivery performance reports, and business forecasts for authorized owners and administrators.
  • Send transactional emails, such as staff approval notices and password-reset messages.
  • Protect the security, integrity, and availability of the Service.
  • Comply with applicable legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

3. Legal basis for processing

We process personal information in accordance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Depending on the activity, we rely on your consent, the performance of a contract or order, our legitimate business interests in operating and securing the Service, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We may share information only as needed to operate the Service:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud for authentication, database storage, hosting, and app infrastructure. Data may be processed on Google Cloud systems in accordance with Google’s security and data-processing terms.
  • Resend for transactional email delivery, such as staff approval and password-reset messages.
  • Courier and delivery partners when necessary to deliver an order or resolve a delivery issue.
  • Marketplace platforms such as Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop when necessary to reconcile or track an order placed through those platforms.
  • Professional advisers, authorities, or courts when required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.
  • Successors in a business transfer if the business or Service is transferred, merged, or reorganized, subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and related Google Cloud services. We use technical and organizational safeguards, including role-based access rules, authenticated access, and encrypted connections, to protect personal information. Access to customer and operational records is restricted to authorized staff, administrators, and owners based on their role.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. While we work to protect personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We retain account information while the account remains active and for a reasonable period afterward. Order, delivery, return, and financial records may be retained for as long as required by tax, accounting, commercial, or other legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymize it in accordance with our retention practices.

If you request deletion, we will process the request within 30 days, subject to records we are legally required to keep. Backup copies may take additional time to expire.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal information is processed.
  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Object to processing in certain circumstances.
  • Request erasure or blocking of information in certain circumstances.
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To exercise these rights, contact us at artlorence.hveridiano@gmail.com. Please include “Privacy Request” in the subject line and enough information for us to verify your identity and locate your account.

You can also review the account deletion process on our Account Deletion page.

8. Children’s privacy

The Service is intended for business operations and is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. International processing

Our service providers, including Google Cloud, may process information in countries other than the Philippines. Where this occurs, we rely on appropriate contractual and organizational safeguards consistent with applicable data-protection law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the Service, our practices, or legal requirements. The effective date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last updated. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or by email.
